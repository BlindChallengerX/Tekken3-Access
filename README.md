# Tekken 3 Access

A screen reader mod for the Tekken 3 PC port
([Tekken3-PC](https://github.com/Muqaddas12/Tekken3-PC)). It speaks through your
screen reader (NVDA, JAWS and others), or the Windows voice if none is running,
and adds sounds so blind players can fight by ear.

**Download:** [the latest release](https://github.com/BlindChallengerX/Tekken3-Access/releases/latest)
(Tekken3Access-1.0.4.zip).

## What you need

- The free Tekken 3 PC port: https://github.com/Muqaddas12/Tekken3-PC
- Your own Tekken 3 (USA) PlayStation disc image (.bin and .cue). The mod does
  not include the game.

[INSTALL.txt](INSTALL.txt) walks through the setup step by step.
[MOD-README.txt](MOD-README.txt) explains every key, sound and controller button.

## Highlights

- Every menu, character select, move lists, results and record name entry read aloud.
- A soft distance hum in one-on-one fights, sidestep sounds, and "forward is left / right".
- Tekken Force: each enemy beeps from its side, faster as it closes in; health items ring a bell.
- Tekken Ball playable by ear: ball pings for side and height, a hum that guides you to
  the spot, and three beats that tell you when to swing.
- The opening and every character's ending are audio described in a natural voice, timed
  to the movie, with the game's sound dipping under each line. Theater mode is read too.
- A music volume control (the game has none), and a volume control for the distance hum.
- Xbox and PlayStation controllers, with L3 / R3 for health.

## Reporting problems

Please open an issue (or post on the forum) with `T3Access\T3Access.log` and
`T3Access\launch.log`, and say what you were doing.
